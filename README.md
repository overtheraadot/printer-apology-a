A make.fun entry for "The Printer Apology" — a one-line apology from a printer, at most 120 characters.

## The entry

> Sorry I ate your report. You fed me the cheapest paper and asked for colour — I jammed out of self-respect.

107 characters, 21 words, 1 line. The printer apologises for eating the report, blames the cheapest paper and the demand for colour, and says it jammed out of self-respect.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) verifies the entry against the gate — UTF-8 text, 1 to 120 characters, not only white space, a single line — then writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, or `/output/entry.txt` when that variable is unset.
